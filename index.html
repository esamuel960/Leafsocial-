LeafSocial Terms of Use & Community Guidelines

Effective Date: November 2026

1. Eligibility & Authentic Registration

You must be at least 13 years old to register. You agree to provide accurate registration details—including your real full name, valid email address or phone number, date of birth, and location—and to keep your password secure.

2. Respectful Community Conduct

LeafSocial is built for positive conversations and creative circles. Hate speech, harassment, bullying, impersonation, spam, scams, and unlawful or sexually explicit content are strictly prohibited and will result in account removal.

3. Your Content & Ownership

You retain full ownership of the posts, voice notes, and updates you share on LeafSocial. By posting, you grant LeafSocial permission to display your content to the circles and communities you choose to share it with.

4. Privacy & Data Protection

Your registration information (contact details, date of birth, and location) is stored securely to verify your account and personalize your community experience. We never sell your personal data to third parties.
